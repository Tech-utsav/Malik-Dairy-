Your neighbourhood dairy & supermarket

Malik Dairy is a local supermarket and dairy store serving the Smalkha Village area. We stock fresh milk, curd, paneer, butter and ghee, along with sweets, cold drinks, packaged snacks, biscuits, groceries and other daily-use products.

Run by Rakesh Malik, the shop is where families come for the morning milk and stay for everything else on the list. One stop, one bill, and it's open until 9 PM every day.
